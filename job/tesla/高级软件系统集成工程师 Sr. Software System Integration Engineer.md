高级软件系统集成工程师 Sr. Software System Integration Engineer

硬件测试工程 Hardware Test Engineering
申请职位
职位描述
The Role

The person will work as member of Hardware Test Engineering team in GFSH, you will apply software skills to integrate hardware and software by working cross-functionally with internal and external teams. You will have the opportunity to work on various parts of the system and wear multiple hats, from designing system architecture to test automation and debugging/commissioning systems, you will also be part of global test system engineering team to accelerate those applications in Tesla local RD labs.


Responsibilities

Research, develop, create, and execute feature requests or effective solutions end-to-end (hardware/software).
Identify, escalate and resolve issues with hardware and software/firmware during system bring-up, performance and safety tests.
Develop and validate Python code and embedded firmware in C, C++.
Lead software/system integration efforts for all test platforms.
Lead development/integration effort for our in-house and off -the shelf bi-directional power supplies.
Serve as a technical consultant for feature requests from cross-functional teams, adopt in-field requirements or specification to test system localization.
Contribute to our internal software tool repositories.
Collaborate with technician and/or 3rd party lab resources.


Requirements

Degree in Electrical, Computer or Mechanical Engineering or equivalent experience.
Strong knowledge of high voltage power electronics systems, electric motors, and low voltage systems (dynamometers experience is a plus).
Strong knowledge of test automation and data analysis in Python.
Familiarity with embedded system/firmware in C/C++.
Motivation to work hands-on in solving programs efficiently to meet tight deadlines
Experience with a multitude of laboratory test equipment, including sensors, oscilloscopes, power analyzers, and other instrumentation.
Familiarity with communication interfaces such as CAN/CAN FD, LIN, Modbus, SCPI, Automotive Ethernet, EtherCAT, etc.
Strong language skill both written and oral English
Skills to learn, self-motivation
